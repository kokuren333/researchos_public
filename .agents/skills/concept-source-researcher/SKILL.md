# Concept source researcher

Use this skill before writing or substantially revising a Concept. Do not draft from general knowledge first.

## Required sequence

1. Read the Concept Contract and identify the claims the page must support.
2. Discover at least three independent, authoritative sources when the Concept is a priority Concept.
3. Open and read the relevant source sections; a URL alone is not evidence.
4. Record an Evidence item with a stable ID, source ID, precise locator, and `extracted_support` summarising what was actually read.
5. Construct claims only from the extracted evidence and record Claim → Evidence → Source links.
6. Draft the Concept only after the packet is complete enough to support the intended sections.

## Grounding rules

- Do not use generated prose as evidence.
- Do not upgrade a locator-only record to grounded.
- Weaken or remove claims that the read source does not support.
- Keep uncertainty and scope limits explicit.
- Public prose uses numbered citations; internal IDs remain hidden from readers.

For `measurement.validity`, cover interpretation/use, construct and operationalization, reliability, converging evidence, context transport, proxy/error, and downstream statistical or causal consequences. Preserve the packet as the authoring record.
