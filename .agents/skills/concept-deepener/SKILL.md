# concept-deepener

Rewrite a Concept draft into a teachable page: definition, intuition, importance, example, misuse, boundaries, research practice, prerequisites, related Concepts, and source locator. Preserve uncertainty and do not broaden claims beyond the sources.

## Claim expansion rule

Mentioning a required claim is not coverage. For each required claim, expand the learning unit with: why it is true, what related Concept it must be distinguished from, a concrete example, a counterexample or boundary, and a practical research implication. Connect the units into an argument rather than appending a checklist.

For `measurement.validity`, explicitly teach construct / operationalization / measurement, reliability versus validity, converging validity evidence, population/context/purpose dependence, proxy risk, measurement error, reference standards, and downstream statistical or causal consequences. Include clinical, physical-measurement, and non-medical transfer examples.
